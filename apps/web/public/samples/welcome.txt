Welcome to CloudNest.

A home for your files.

This is an interactive local demo, with sample originals you can download. Uploads are saved in this browser, not sent to Telegram. Clearing browser data can remove local originals.

Create folders, mark favorites, add tags, and find what matters. Trash is reversible. Export metadata from Settings.

A live Telegram connection requires a configured TDLib worker and verified feasibility tests. This independent third-party client is not affiliated with Telegram.
